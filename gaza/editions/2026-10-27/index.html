Limited-source update / October 27, 2026

Gaza: girl dies after gunfire wounds as camp fire displaces families

WAFA reported on October 27 that a six-year-old girl died Tuesday morning from wounds sustained in an earlier shooting in al-Mawasi, Khan Younis. The report does not specify when the shooting occurred.

WAFA reported on October 26 that a displacement-camp fire near the Shuja’iyya junction in eastern Gaza City, described as occurring the previous night, displaced more than 800 Palestinians and affected more than 200 families. A separate WAFA report said two Palestinians were killed and another wounded in a drone strike on Deir al-Balah on October 26.

Limited-source update based on three WAFA reports. Normal provider collection was blocked before fetching, so this is not a complete daily briefing. This edition is text-only.
