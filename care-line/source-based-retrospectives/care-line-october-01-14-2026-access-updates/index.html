The Care Line Dispatch

Access updates from October 1-14, 2026

This dated retrospective preserves what the cited reports said on their stated dates. It is not a claim that every condition remained current after those reports, and it does not claim complete coverage of healthcare access changes.

Reported October 1, 2026 · Fluvanna County, Virginia

Volunteer EMS authority was still suspended in the September 29 status described by the October 1 report

Fluvanna Review's October 1 report described volunteer emergency medical services authority as still suspended on September 29 in Palmyra, Kents Store and Fork Union after a September 11 suspension. County-paid EMS remained operational, and the report said the suspension could last up to 45 days, with earlier restoration possible. This entry does not establish the status after September 29.

Sources: Fluvanna Review, October 1; September 24 comparison report.

Reported October 13, 2026 · Omaha, Nebraska

Hickory Villa closure plan set resident moves before October 16

Flatwater Free Press reported that Bethesda planned to close Hickory Villa and move residents before October 16. This entry records the announced plan; it does not assert that the closure was completed or state where residents ultimately moved. Hickory Villa is separate from Cambridge Court, which closed in September.

Sources: Flatwater Free Press, October 13; KMTV comparison report.

Patient notice dated October 14, 2026 · Winchester, New Hampshire

Winchester Family Medicine announced a planned permanent closure

An official Cheshire Medical Center notice announced that the Winchester Family Medicine office at 20 Warwick Road would permanently close and said its sole provider, Lisa Moulton, would leave on November 6. The first patient letter was mailed October 14 and replacement-provider details would follow. This entry does not claim that patient transfers were completed or establish the sequence of closure and departure.

Source: Cheshire Medical Center official notice.

Reported October 14, 2026 · Washington County, Maine

Dennys River Ambulance planned an end-of-November shutdown

Maine Public reported that Dennys River Ambulance Service planned to close at the end of November after more than 60 years, with insufficient volunteers cited as a reason. DownEast EMS was expected to cover some unorganized territories, while replacement arrangements for Dennysville and Pembroke were uncertain in the report. A longer response time is not presented here as a measured outcome.

Source: Maine Public, October 14.

CMS notice dated October 9, 2026 · California · CCN 059525

CMS planned to terminate Tender Home Health's Medicare provider agreement

CMS listed a planned October 24 termination of Tender Home Health's Medicare provider agreement for substantial noncompliance. This is a provider-participation action, not evidence that the business or facility closed, and this entry does not claim that the termination became effective.

Sources: CMS public notice; CMS provider notice index.
