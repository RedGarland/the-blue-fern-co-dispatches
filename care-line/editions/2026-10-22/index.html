Virginia Medicaid coverage changes take effect for specified gender-dysphoria services

Virginia's Department of Medical Assistance Services said coverage for specified gender-dysphoria services ended for Medicaid members under 18 and CHIP members under 19 for dates of service beginning Oct. 13, 2026, under a federal rule. The rule permits up to six months of continued federal participation only for existing cross-sex hormone-therapy recipients. Mental-health coverage remains unchanged.

Scope

  • DMAS's age boundary is Medicaid members under 18; the CHIP boundary is members under 19.
  • The Federal Register rule's continuation provision is limited to existing cross-sex hormone-therapy recipients and does not establish the same exception for puberty blockers or surgery.
  • The rule concerns federal Medicaid and CHIP funding; this report does not claim that all private or state-only coverage ended.
