Dated backfill: food-access pressure reports from October 13–17, 2026

These are dated retrospective reports. Their report dates are October 13, October 15, and October 17, 2026; they describe those dated developments and are not new October 20 events.

October 17, 2026 · Minnesota

Minnesota food shelves struggle to keep pace with rising visits

MPR News reported October 17 that Minnesota food shelves faced accelerating visits and extended hours without keeping pace with demand; Second Harvest Heartland said rising freight costs led it to trim other spending and reduce some produce purchases.

Source: MPR News · Published October 17, 2026

October 15, 2026 · Georgia and South Carolina

Golden Harvest faces increased need as donated and federal food declines

WRDW/WAGT reported October 15 that Golden Harvest served 24 counties in Georgia and South Carolina amid increased need, empty warehouses, and reduced donated and federal commodity food.

Source: WRDW/WAGT · Published October 15, 2026

October 13, 2026 · Retrospective pantry reporting

Food pantries report rising visits and shrinking reserves

PBS NewsHour reported October 13 that FeedMore saw visits rise 20% over the preceding year, while Moments of Hope saw visits double over two years and reserves fall from about two months at the start of the year to two weeks.

Source: PBS NewsHour · Published October 13, 2026
