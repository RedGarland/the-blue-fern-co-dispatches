Event October 17, 2026 · Laredo, Texas

Emiliano Andrade-Covarrubias died in ICE detention in Laredo

The Texas Tribune reported that Emiliano Andrade-Covarrubias, 56, died at the Rio Grande Processing Center in Laredo on October 17. The report cited his daughter and Rep. Henry Cuellar; Cuellar said staff found him unresponsive and attempted resuscitation. Family allegations about medical care are attributed, and the report did not establish a cause of death.

Source: The Texas Tribune, published October 18 and updated October 19.

Complaint filed October 1, 2026 · Minneapolis, Minnesota

Federal complaint filed over a January Minneapolis shooting involving an ICE officer

On October 1, Brent Ganger, acting as trustee for Renée Good's next of kin, and Rebecca Good filed a federal complaint against the United States under the Federal Tort Claims Act over a January 7 Minneapolis shooting involving an ICE officer. The complaint's allegations and requested relief are not judicial findings, and this entry does not present the filing as a new October enforcement event.

Source: filed complaint, case 0:26-cv-04230, Doc. 1.
