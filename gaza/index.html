November 11 OCHA appeal for Gaza winter supplies

In a November 11 statement, UN relief chief Tom Fletcher urged immediate entry of winter supplies into Gaza, wider crossing access, funding, and conditions for humanitarian organizations and commercial markets to operate. He identified durable shelter, bedding, safe heating, fuel and equipment for floodwater, debris and explosive-ordnance clearance as needs. This is an appeal, not confirmation that supplies arrived. | 2 sources
