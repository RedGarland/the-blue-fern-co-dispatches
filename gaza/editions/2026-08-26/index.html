The Guardian · conflict · Gaza · August 26, 2026

Efforts to rebuild domestic food production face severe challenges and have come at deadly cost As Gaza's population of 2.1 million has been hemmed into an ever smaller slice of the territory, farmers are using every last remaining patch of unspoiled earth, no matter how tiny, in their struggle to survive.
