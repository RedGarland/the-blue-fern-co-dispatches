AFP reports eight deaths in November 1 Gaza strikes

Event date: 2026-11-01; source published: 2026-11-01

AFP reported on November 1 that Gaza emergency services and hospitals said Israeli strikes killed eight people, including a child: five in Khan Younis and three in Gaza City. These are attributed reports from the two locations, not an independently established full-day death toll.

Attribution: AFP via Arab News

Uncertainty: Eight includes five plus three; do not add to WAFA ten-since-dawn. No exact child age or all-civilian/combatant classification. No verified military response established in retained source review. Different publishers may share underlying hospital sources.

Reuters reports four deaths in November 2 Gaza airstrikes

Event date: 2026-11-02; source published: 2026-11-02

Reuters reported at least four Palestinians killed in Israeli airstrikes on November 2: two in Khan Younis, one in Al-Mussadar and one in a later vehicle strike in Gaza City's Tel al-Hawa neighborhood. Israel's military said it conducted two strikes targeting militants, without identifying the targets, and did not immediately comment on the vehicle strike. WAFA reported a corresponding four-incident breakdown. The accounts are not additive and do not establish a final daily toll.

Attribution: Reuters via ThePrint

Uncertainty: Exclude Yousef Abu Younis earlier-wounds death because antecedent event conflicts across WAFA reports. Do not assert all four victims were civilians or that military targets match specific fatalities. Later Reuters four-death version supersedes earlier three-death version; syndicated copies are one origin. Separate evening gunfire is excluded from this item, not silently included in a day total.

Sources

  • english.wafa.ps

Xinhua reports November 3 Rafah travel and shipment suspension

Event date: 2026-11-03; source published: 2026-11-04

Xinhua reported that Israeli authorities turned travelers back at Rafah and suspended humanitarian and commercial shipments from Egypt on November 3. Gaza's crossings authority said the change came without prior notice. Israeli military sources cited by Kan attributed the closure to alleged smuggling and interference by Hamas security personnel; those sources said Kerem Shalom was unaffected. The report does not establish that every Gaza aid crossing closed or that the suspension continued on later dates.

Attribution: Xinhua

Uncertainty: EventNov3, sourceNov4; do not fabricate a Nov4 event or convert unknown timezone. Keep nested Israeli-military-sources-via-Kan attribution and Kerem Shalom exception. No continued closure claim for Nov4 or later. Omit roughly40-truck mixed aid/commercial figure to avoid needless scope ambiguity.

Sources

  • english.news.cn

UN warns restrictions limit Gaza waste collection

Event date: 2026-11-03; source published: 2026-11-03

Anadolu reported on November 3 that UN spokesperson Stephane Dujarric said only half of humanitarian partners' waste-collection vehicle fleet in Gaza was operational because of Israeli restrictions on engine oil and spare parts. He warned that incomplete rubbish collection was increasing public-health risks and called for wider entry of essential supplies. This records the UN's assessment of ongoing conditions; the underlying assessment period was not specified.

Attribution: UN spokesperson Stephane Dujarric, reported by Anadolu; assessment period unspecified

Uncertainty: Half refers to humanitarian partners fleet, not all municipal waste vehicles. Nov3 statement date is not crisis onset; earlier Oct2 reporting already described grounded vehicles. Anadolu and transcript reproduce one statement, not independent field measurements. No quantified deterioration over an earlier comparable baseline.

Sources

  • transcripts.un.org
