Joint Foreign Ministers Statement on Gaza

UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office · aid_access_and_diplomacy · Gaza Strip · November 15, 2026

In a November 15 joint statement, the foreign ministers of Australia, Canada, Italy and the United Kingdom called for immediate humanitarian access and winter supplies to enter Gaza.

They said 150,000 tents and other supplies were held at the border, called for crossings to open and necessary dual-use items to enter, and urged all parties to respect the ceasefire and international humanitarian law.

This reports the ministers' appeal and attributed claim, not independently verified stocks or confirmed aid delivery.
