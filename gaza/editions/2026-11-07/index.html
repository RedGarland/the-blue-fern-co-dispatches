Core Gaza Developments

Anadolu reports storm injuries and collapses in Gaza

Anadolu Agency · shelter_and_relief · Gaza Strip · November 7, 2026

Anadolu reported on November 7 that medical sources said five Palestinians were injured in separate storm-related incidents involving two wall collapses and falling corrugated metal in northern and central Gaza.

Civil Defence spokesperson Mahmoud Basal separately described four collapses involving homes damaged by previous Israeli strikes, including partial and roof collapses, with no injuries reported in those four incidents.

The report also described rain flooding displacement camps and shelters. These attributed accounts should not be combined into one casualty total.

ICSPR calls for shelter and emergency equipment after Gaza rains

ICSPR · humanitarian_access · Gaza Strip · November 7, 2026

In a statement dated November 7, Palestinian rights organization ICSPR called for weather-resistant shelters, bedding, pumps, rescue equipment and fuel, and support for municipal and emergency services.

It warned that poor drainage and sanitation could worsen disease risks. These are the organization's assessments and requests, not evidence that supplies had been delivered or a disease outbreak had occurred.

The statement responds to the same storm described in the accompanying reporting.
