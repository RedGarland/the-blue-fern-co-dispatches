UNICEF reports winter flood mitigation and shelter support in Gaza

UNICEF · humanitarian · Gaza · November 27, 2026

UNICEF reported that flood-mitigation machinery reached 129 displacement sites housing 21,145 families. It said 10,530 tarpaulins reached an estimated 5,265 families, a separate tally that may overlap.

It reported receiving 43,000 sandbags; that is not a count of installed sandbags. Additional water pumps were awaiting entry approval.

Gaza Civil Defense warns deteriorating shelters face flood risk

Anadolu Ajansi · humanitarian · Gaza · November 28, 2026

Gaza Civil Defense spokesman Mahmoud Basal told reporters that severe weather could flood some 200,000 deteriorating tents and 1,000 shelters. These are estimates of exposure, not reports of new flood damage.

Basal said responders were short of fuel, spare parts, vehicles and water pumps.
