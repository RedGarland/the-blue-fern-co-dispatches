Father, child among eight Palestinians killed in Israeli attacks on Gaza

Al Jazeera · conflict · Gaza · November 10, 2026

Al Jazeera reports, citing local health sources, that Israeli attacks killed at least eight Palestinians on 10 November.

It describes three deaths in an attack on a home in Daraj, a father and his 13-year-old son killed in an attack on a tent in Zeitoun, and a man killed by gunfire in Khan Younis.

The article's later update cites Wafa reporting two further deaths in an attack in Sabra. These are attributed reports of separate incidents, not an independently verified final daily toll. They are not added to cumulative ceasefire or storm casualties.

Board of Peace says Israel's aid response after Gaza floods 'insufficent'

BBC News · conflict · Gaza · November 10, 2026

BBC reported on 10 November that the Board of Peace called for Israel to allow necessary humanitarian supplies into Gaza after flooding and described the response as insufficient.

The Board also blamed Hamas's failure to disarm and transfer authority for compounding hardship. COGAT said tents and winter supplies had entered Gaza and maintained restrictions on equipment it said could have military uses.

Hamas disputed the Board's account and blamed Israeli restrictions. These are the parties' stated positions, not an independently established allocation of responsibility.

Humanitarian Situation Report: Heavy Rains in the Gaza Strip | 8 November 2026

OCHA · displacement · Gaza · November 9, 2026

OCHA's report titled 8 November, published on 9 November, describes rain beginning on 6 November and damage over that weekend to displacement sites, shelters and services across Gaza.

Humanitarian partners reported more than 26,000 household appeals by 8 November; OCHA identifies the figures as preliminary. OCHA says Israeli restrictions on supplies and equipment and funding shortages constrain the response.

This is a weekend assessment, not a new measurement on 10 November.

Reports concern one sandbank collapse at a displacement camp south of Deir al-Balah on 2026-11-08. Accounts and casualty figures are attributed separately below; they are not added together.
