Israel kills three Palestinians in Gaza attacks

Middle East Eye · conflict · Gaza · June 28, 2026

Israeli attacks have killed at least three Palestinians in the Gaza Strip, including two people targeted by a drone in the north of the enclave.

Palestinian health authorites said an Israeli drone struck the al-Salatin area, west of Beit Lahia, killing two people and wounding at least one more. In southern Gaza, Israeli forces also bombed a tent sheltering displaced Palestinians in Khan Younis.

An Israeli warplane fired a missile at the tent, wounding several people who were taken to hospital for treatment. Medical staff at Nasser Medical Complex said they received the body of one unidentified person after the attack on the tent.

Earlier, health officials in Gaza said. Israel's war on the enclave had killed at least 73,054 Palestinians and wounded 173,480 since October 7, 2023. They added that hospitals had received three bodies and treated 43 wounded people over the past 24 hours.

Gaza dialysis patients at risk as machines fail

Middle East Eye · conflict · Gaza · June 28, 2026

Nearly half of the dialysis machines at Gaza's largest hospital have stopped working as. Israel's blockade and repeated attacks continue to cripple the enclave's health system.

Dr Ghazi al-Yaziji, head of the artificial kidney department at al-Shifa Medical Complex, said a severe shortage of sodium bicarbonate, a substance needed to control dangerous acidity in the blood of kidney failure patients, has forced 25 machines out of service.

He said only 26 machines are now operating, and even those are working only partially. Medical teams have had to shorten dialysis sessions from four hours to three and cut weekly treatment sessions from three to two, al-Yaziji said.

He warned that the cuts are putting about 240 patients with Stage 5 chronic kidney failure at serious risk. The reduced treatment has increased the danger of heart and respiratory complications, which could prove fatal in some cases, he added.
