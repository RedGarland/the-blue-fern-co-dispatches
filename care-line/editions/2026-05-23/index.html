Core Healthcare Access Signals

Hospital closure

Medicaid cuts threaten hundreds of hospitals, new report finds

Herald-Standard | Hospital closure | Pennsylvania | 2026-05-07

What changed: A new report warned that Medicaid cuts could threaten hundreds of hospitals.

Who may be affected: Patients, rural communities, and hospital staff in Pennsylvania.

Why it matters: Hospital financing pressure can reduce access even before a formal closure occurs.

Limit: This is a reported risk signal, not a measured closure count.

Hospital / Clinic Operations Signals

Clinic access strain

River Hills Community Health Center announces closure of Centerville clinic

KCRG | Clinic access strain | Centerville, Iowa | 2026-05-12

What changed: River Hills Community Health Center announced the closure of its Centerville clinic.

Who may be affected: Clinic patients in and around Centerville.

Why it matters: A local clinic closure can mean longer travel, fewer appointment options, or delayed routine care.

Limit: The article does not quantify total patient displacement.

Maternity / Family Care Signals

Maternity care loss

Patients worry after Los Alamos Medical Center halts labor, delivery services

Searchlight New Mexico | Maternity care loss | Los Alamos, New Mexico | 2026-05-17

What changed: Los Alamos Medical Center halted labor and delivery services.

Who may be affected: Pregnant patients, families, and patients needing local maternity care near Los Alamos.

Why it matters: Loss of local labor and delivery services can force patients to travel farther for time-sensitive care.

Limit: The article focuses on one facility and does not measure regional demand.
