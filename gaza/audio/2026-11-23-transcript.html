This is the Gaza Dispatch audio briefing for November 23, 2026.

Here are the key source-backed developments from today's edition.

1. Four Palestinians killed, including UNRWA employee, in Israeli attacks across Gaza. WAFA reported that four Palestinians, including UNRWA employee Adel Sami al-Helou, were killed in Israeli attacks and gunfire across Gaza since dawn; it said three Palestinians were killed when Israeli aircraft targeted a civilian vehicle near Ansar Roundabout west of Gaza City, with several others injured. This was reported by WAFA.

2. Gaza child dies from lead poisoning, medical sources confirm. WAFA reported that medical sources in Gaza announced a child's death from acute lead poisoning and linked the case to unsafe battery repair in displacement camps and tents, while noting that limited supplies and testing capacity may leave other cases undiagnosed. This was reported by WAFA.
