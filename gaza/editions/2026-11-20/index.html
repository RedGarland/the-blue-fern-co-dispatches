Italy and UN agencies partner to strengthen integrated services for children and young people across the Gaza Strip

UNICEF · humanitarian_conditions · Gaza · November 20, 2026

UNICEF reported that an official Jerusalem launch on Nov. 20 announced a EUR 10 million, 18-month Gaza child-services programme.

UNICEF said existing activity at four centres is already serving over 10,000 school-age children; more than 28,500 is a targeted reach across the named groups, not achieved reach. The programme had already begun, so this is not described as a new start.
