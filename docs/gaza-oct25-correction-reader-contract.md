This source change grants no publication, activation, merge, or runner-installation authority. It adds a narrowly bound correction profile and private verification tools. It must not be described as scheduled production recovery.

## Inactive and active history

An ordinary checkout without a committed activation context retains legacy history semantics. Pending corrected bytes cannot activate themselves: they cause refusal, not approval. Within a verified active context, every present Oct25 replica must be complete and exact. Exact original replicas remain archival evidence; they never replace the narrowed retained row. A corrected replica is projected once regardless of root iteration order. Missing, partial, unknown, linked, or conflicting evidence refuses. Persisted original memory remains archived but is excluded from active corrected selection.

The profile does not invent scoring, category, ground flags, casualty counts, or newness. Publisher source identity remains separate from editorial presentation. Audio and social generation for this site-only correction are denied before fallback, credentials, or rendering.

## Immutable proof and private packages

The verifier binds the exact proposal, approval-only child, evidence inventory, public paths, and hashes. Public source contains no private consent or review-packet digest. A future private trust-provider design requires separate semantic/privacy review. Public content hashes are integrity constraints, never a substitute for authority. No real consent, proposal, audit, approval, or registry artifact is distributed in this PR.

The current ancestry-based activation verifier is suitable for isolated private evidence review. Its private approval ancestry must NOT be merged or pushed into public source. A future runtime deployment requires a separately reviewed private-evidence/reference design, compatible protected-source synchronization, and explicit installation authority.
