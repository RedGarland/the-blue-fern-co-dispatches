Palestinian American woman held without charge by Israeli military

The Guardian · conflict · Gaza · June 13, 2026

Sama Safi, a psychology student at Birzeit University in the occupied West Bank, has not been charged with any crimes.

A spokesperson for the Israeli military said she and three other women detained around the same time were arrested "after promoting hostile terrorist activity and additional terrorist-related activities".
